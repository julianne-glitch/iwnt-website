# Product Marketing Context

*Last updated: 2026-09-15*

## Product Overview
**One-liner:** IWNT (Intel Workforce Network Technologies Ltd) is building a connected workforce-operations platform for organisations hiring and managing teams across African markets.
**What it does:** Hire, onboard, manage, and pay coordination across African markets, with local delivery via IntelHRC group operations.
**Product category:** Workforce operations / HR technology for Africa
**Product type:** SaaS platform (first release in development)
**Business model:** Pre-seed. Partners invited. Not claiming IWNT-owned EOR entities.

## Legal / company facts
- Legal name: Intel Workforce Network Technologies Ltd
- Founded: 2026, DIFC (Innovation One), Dubai
- CEO: Derick Fonderson
- Contact: info@iwnt.ae

## Target Audience
**Target companies:** Firms in Dubai and globally that want African workforce access without building local entities from scratch.
**Decision-makers:** Founders, COOs, HR/expansion leads, partnership teams.
**Primary use case:** Plug into African markets through software + IntelHRC operations.
**Jobs to be done:**
- Enter or scale hiring in African markets
- Partner early / request a pilot
- Use a tech layer instead of another broker

## Differentiation
IntelHRC owns operating entities in 8 African countries. IWNT builds the software. Pre-seed MVP, DIFC Dubai HQ, open to partners.

## Words to use
Technology company, IntelHRC, marketplace/platform, pre-seed, partners invited, 8 African markets / group footprint, DIFC.

## Words to avoid
IWNT-owned entities, “we own 8 entities”, finished live payroll execution, fabricated customer volumes, overstated AWS workloads.
